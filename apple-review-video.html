How to record the SafePulse demo video

Please read this whole page before you start. Every step matters. If something does not work, stop and send me a message — do not keep recording.

Most important thing on this page

The alert message will arrive in WhatsApp. It will NOT arrive in the Messages app.

This is normal. My system sends a normal SMS only to United States and Canada numbers. For a Pakistan number it sends a WhatsApp message instead. So please watch WhatsApp, not Messages, when you wait for the alert.

1 What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or newer. It should be your own phone, so you can use it for a few days.
  • WhatsApp installed on that iPhone and signed in.
  • An email address you can open on the phone.
  • The TestFlight app (free, from the App Store).
  • About 30 minutes of quiet time.

You only need one phone. You will add your own phone number as the emergency contact, so the messages come back to the same phone. That is fine and it is what we want.

2 Install the app

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open TestFlight and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that I sent the invite to. If you use a different Apple ID, TestFlight will show a "Redeem Code" screen and the app will not appear.
  3. Open the invite link I sent you. Tap Accept, then tap Install.
  4. Look at the top of the TestFlight screen. Send me the version and build number before you do anything else. It should say 2.3.3 and build 171 or higher.

3 Test first — do not record yet

Please do this test without recording. It only takes a few minutes, and it makes sure everything works before you spend time on the video.

  1. Open the app and finish the short setup. Type your name and your phone number.
  2. Go to Contacts and tap Add contact.
  3. Type any name. For the phone number, type your own number in full international format, like +923001234567. Do not type 03001234567.
  4. Also type a real email address. The app will ask for it and will not let you save without it. This is normal for Pakistan numbers, it is not an error.
  5. Save the contact. Tap Send invite. The Messages app opens with the text already written. Tap Send.
  6. The message arrives on your own phone. Tap the link, scroll down, and tap Yes / I Agree.
  7. Go back to the app. The contact should now say Approved.
  8. Tap the red SOS button.
  9. Open WhatsApp and check your email inbox.
Then send me a message and tell me: what arrived, which app it arrived in (WhatsApp or email or both), and how many minutes it took.
If nothing arrives, stop. Do not record the video. Send me a message and I will fix it first. Recording before this test works is how a lot of time was wasted last time.

4 Three things that will spoil the video

  • Looking in Messages for the alert. The alert comes to WhatsApp. Only the first invite comes to Messages.
  • Typing the number as 03... It must start with +92. A number starting with 0 cannot be delivered.
  • Skipping the email. For a Pakistan number the email is required. Put in a real one you can open.

5 Record these 9 clips

Record each one as a separate video. Name them 01, 02, 03 and so on. Use the iPhone screen recorder (swipe down from the top-right corner, then tap the record button).

Before you start: delete the contact you made during the test, so you can add it again on camera. If the contact is already approved, the video will not show the approval steps, and those steps are the most important part.
ClipWhat to do
01Open the app. Do the first-time setup. Type your name and your phone number.
02Open Settings and scroll down slowly. Then go to the Home screen and scroll slowly. This shows there is no "Buy", "Upgrade" or "Subscribe" button anywhere in the app. Go slowly so every line can be read.
03Go to Contacts, tap Add contact, and stop. Wait 4 full seconds on the message that says the contact's name and number will be sent to the server. This is the most important shot in the whole video.

05Tap Send invite. The Messages app opens with the text already written. Tap Send. It must be clear that you sent it from your own phone.
06The message arrives. Tap the link. Scroll down. Tap Yes / I Agree. Show the confirmation page.
07Go back to the app. Show that the contact now says Approved.
08Go to the Home screen. Choose 30 minutes. Tap CHECK IN. Show the timer counting down.
09Tap the red SOS button. Open WhatsApp. Show the alert message and let it stay on screen for a few seconds so it can be read. Then tap the map link and show the map opening.

6 Rules while recording

  • Turn on Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode, so no other notifications appear on screen.
  • Never let the phone lock and never show yourself typing your passcode.
  • Do not hide the status bar or the clock at the top of the screen.
  • Move slowly. It is better to be slow and clear than fast.
  • If you make a mistake in one clip, just record that one clip again. You do not need to start over.

7 Send me the files

  • Send the 9 separate video files, with the numbers in the file names.
  • Use Google Drive or WeTransfer. Do not use email — the files are too big.
  • Do not edit them yet. Send the raw recordings first so I can check them.

8 If you are also editing the video

This video is for Apple's review team. It is not an advertisement. One reviewer will watch it to check that the app works the way we say it does.

  • Join the 9 clips in number order. Cut out only the dead time at the start and end of each clip.
  • Add the caption text I send you. Please use my exact words. Do not rewrite them.
  • Export MP4, vertical, 1080 × 1920, about 2 minutes long.

Please do NOT:

  • Add music, an intro, a logo animation, stock footage, transitions or effects.
  • Speed up or shorten any clip to make the app look faster. Apple checks the real timing, and edited timing looks fake.
  • Crop or cut off the top of the screen.
Plain and simple is correct here. A boring video is a good video for this job.

9 Questions

If anything on this page is unclear, or something on the phone does not look like the description here, please message me before you record. It is much faster to fix it first than to record twice.
