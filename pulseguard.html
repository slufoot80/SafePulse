Your Safety, Always On

PulseGuard is a personal safety check-in app. Set a timer, and if you don't check in, PulseGuard alerts your trusted contacts with your location.

How PulseGuard Works

PulseGuard is built for the moments when no one knows where you are. Set a check-in timer before a run, a late drive, a solo hike, or a long day. PulseGuard warns you before the timer runs out — it vibrates and opens the app so you can tap "I'm OK." If you still don't respond, it sends a pre-written SMS (and an optional call) to the emergency contacts you chose — with your last known location — repeating about every 30 minutes until you check in.

Automatic Check-Ins

Choose your interval. PulseGuard runs quietly in the background until you need it.

SOS Button

One tap immediately alerts every emergency contact you've added.

100% Private

All your data stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics.

Low-Battery Warning

If your phone gets dangerously low, PulseGuard warns your circle so they know you may go offline.

Auto-Call on SOS

PulseGuard can automatically dial your first emergency contact when SOS is triggered.

English, 中文, or System Default

Full Simplified Chinese localization. Pick a language in-app, or leave it on System default to follow your iPhone's language setting.
